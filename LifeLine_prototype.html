Eligibility & FAQs

When can I donate again?

After a donation you are hidden for about 3 months. The admin sets the exact period from medical guidelines.

Who sees my phone number?

Only a requester whose request you accept.

What happens to old requests?

They expire automatically after 48 hours unless renewed.
